# DHAIM project status

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-28  
**Public release:** initial research introduction only

This page separates work completed inside the project from claims that are ready to publish as scientific results. It is a dated snapshot, not a declaration that every underlying research document has been released.

## Current boundaries

- **Project framing:** The research questions and architecture are active. The dynamic / integrative / modular shorthand is not a validated unified theory.
- **Dialogue evaluation:** Several internal instrument and pilot tracks exist. They are not presented here as a validated general benchmark.
- **36-response development screen:** The internal archive passed a technical provenance check. That check establishes neither construct validity nor a confirmed frozen scientific interpretation. Model-specific comparisons are not released here as final findings.
- **Relation-aware integration:** A traceable claim/evidence/dependency structure is under development. Its practical advantage over a strong simpler baseline has not been demonstrated.
- **Public artifacts:** No empirical dataset, raw model-output archive, runner, verifier, or comparative result package is included in this initial release.

## What would change this status

A result-focused release requires source and privacy checks, a confirmed scientific closeout with explicit claim limits, and reproducible versions of any released instruments or code. Future updates will identify the exact artifact and date supporting each status change.

Questions, criticism, and relevant prior work are welcome through the repository's Issues page. Please do not interpret an issue discussion, draft, or AI-generated agreement as an approved research conclusion.
